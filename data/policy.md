# IIM Tiruchirappalli — Final Placement Policy 2025-27 (Excerpt)

## 1. Eligibility
All PGDM 2025-27 students in good academic standing (CGPA >= 6.0, no more than 2 active backlogs) are eligible to participate in final placements.

## 2. Dream Company Rule
Every student is allowed **two Dream Company applications** during the placement season. A Dream Company is any recruiter offering a CTC above INR 25 LPA or one designated as "Dream" by the Placement Committee at the season's start. Once a student accepts an offer from a non-Dream company, they may still interview at their two chosen Dream companies but cannot apply to any additional recruiters.

## 3. Offer Acceptance
Any offer extended through the campus process must be accepted within **48 hours** of receipt unless the recruiter explicitly grants extended consideration in writing. Failure to respond within 48 hours is treated as acceptance.

## 4. Slotting and Day Zero
Day Zero companies are announced 72 hours in advance. Students shortlisted for a Day Zero recruiter cannot appear for any other recruiter's process running in the same slot. Slot clashes are resolved in favour of the higher-CTC recruiter unless the student notifies the Committee 24 hours in advance.

## 5. Dress Code
Formal business attire is mandatory for all Pre-Placement Talks (PPTs), Group Discussions, and interviews conducted on campus or virtually.

## 6. Withdrawal
Students may withdraw from any recruiter's process **before the shortlist is released**, without penalty. Withdrawal after shortlist requires written justification and Committee approval; unjustified withdrawals may lead to a one-recruiter debarment.

## 7. Committee Contact
Queries not answered by PlacementOS should be routed to the Placement Office (placements@iimtrichy.ac.in) with your roll number in the subject line.

# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I apply to Google and McKinsey both if they're both Dream companies?**
A: Yes. You have exactly two Dream slots and both may be used on the same day if the schedule permits.

**Q: What happens if I miss a deadline?**
A: Missed application deadlines result in automatic disqualification from that recruiter's process. Extensions are not granted except for medical emergencies with documentation.

**Q: Is prior work experience mandatory?**
A: Not for most recruiters, but consulting firms and product management roles often require 12-24 months of prior full-time experience.

**Q: How is CGPA calculated for eligibility?**
A: Cumulative CGPA at the end of the most recently completed term is used. Provisional grades from the current term are not considered.

**Q: Are backlogs disqualifying?**
A: Depends on the recruiter. Most consulting and finance firms require zero active backlogs. IT services firms typically allow up to two.
